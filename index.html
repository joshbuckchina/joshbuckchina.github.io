About

The author

Josh Buck writes dark fiction about ordinary people caught in extraordinary circumstances, where survival comes at a price and the line between right and wrong is rarely clear.

His stories blend psychological tension, unsettling mysteries, and speculative elements, exploring the choices people make when the systems they trust begin to fail.

Four novels — The Rogue Medic, The Molt, Low Water, and Silent Echoes — plus an illustrated beginner's guide to WorkBuddy. All published on Amazon.
